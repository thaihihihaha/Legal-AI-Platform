# Huong dan su dung ung dung - Legal AI Platform

Tai lieu nay danh cho nguoi dung cuoi (phong Phap che, Nhan su, Van hanh, Quan ly).

## 1. Muc dich ung dung
Ung dung giup doanh nghiep:
- Quan ly hop dong va tai lieu phap ly tap trung.
- Tra cuu phap ly bang AI co dan chieu.
- Tao van ban theo mau nhanh, giam loi thu cong.
- Theo doi rui ro va qua trinh phe duyet.

## 2. Dang nhap va bao mat tai khoan

### 2.1 Dang nhap
1. Mo trang he thong.
2. Nhap email va mat khau.
3. Bam Dang nhap.

Neu he thong yeu cau OTP (2FA):
1. Mo ung dung xac thuc tren dien thoai.
2. Nhap ma OTP 6 so.
3. Xac nhan de vao he thong.

### 2.2 Khuyen nghi bao mat
- Khong chia se mat khau/OTP.
- Doi mat khau dinh ky.
- Dang xuat khi dung may tinh cong cong.
- Bao quan tri vien neu tai khoan co dau hieu bat thuong.

## 3. Tong quan giao dien
Cac khu vuc chinh:
- Dashboard: xem nhanh so lieu hop dong, tai lieu, mau van ban.
- Contracts: upload, cap nhat, review hop dong.
- Documents: quan ly tai lieu noibo.
- Templates: tao van ban theo mau.
- Legal Search: hoi dap/tra cuu phap ly.
- Settings/Profile: API key (neu duoc cap quyen), thong tin ca nhan, doi mat khau, 2FA.

## 4. Huong dan theo tac vu

### 4.1 Upload hop dong va review rui ro
1. Vao Contracts.
2. Chon Upload hop dong.
3. Chon file hop le (docx/pdf theo cau hinh he thong).
4. Sau khi upload xong, mo hop dong vua tai.
5. Bam Review de he thong phan tich rui ro.
6. Doc cac canh bao, diem rui ro va khuyen nghi dieu chinh.

Meo:
- Dat ten file ro rang: loai hop dong + doi tac + ngay.
- Kiem tra metadata de tim kiem nhanh hon.

### 4.2 Quan ly tai lieu
1. Vao Documents.
2. Upload tai lieu noi bo hoac phap ly.
3. Gan danh muc/tag (neu duoc phep).
4. Su dung tim kiem de loc tai lieu theo ten/loai/trang thai.

### 4.3 Tao van ban tu template
1. Vao Templates.
2. Chon loai mau van ban phu hop.
3. Dien cac truong du lieu bat buoc.
4. Bam Generate de tao noi dung.
5. Xem lai va chinh sua.
6. Export ra dinh dang can dung.

Khuyen nghi:
- Kiem tra ten cong ty, thong tin ben lien quan, ngay thang.
- Doc lai dieu khoan quan trong truoc khi phat hanh.

### 4.4 Lam viec voi Draft va phe duyet
1. Tao Draft moi hoac mo Draft co san.
2. Chinh sua noi dung.
3. Bam Validate de kiem tra.
4. Chuyen trang thai sang review.
5. Nguoi co tham quyen phe duyet hoac tu choi.

### 4.5 Tra cuu phap ly voi AI
1. Vao Legal Search.
2. Nhap cau hoi ro rang (boi canh, doi tuong, van de).
3. Gui cau hoi.
4. Doc cau tra loi va dan chieu nguon.
5. Neu can, dat cau hoi tiep de lam ro.

Luu y:
- Cau hoi cu the thi ket qua thuong tot hon.
- Luon doi soat voi quy dinh noi bo truoc khi ra quyet dinh cuoi cung.

## 5. Cac vai tro nguoi dung
Tuy doanh nghiep cau hinh, he thong thuong co:
- Owner/Admin: quan tri nguoi dung, quyen, cau hinh nang cao.
- Member: thuc hien nghiep vu hang ngay (upload, soan thao, tra cuu).
- Viewer: xem du lieu theo pham vi duoc cap.

Neu khong thay nut/chuc nang, kha nang cao la tai khoan chua duoc cap quyen.

## 6. Xu ly su co thuong gap

### 6.1 Khong dang nhap duoc
- Kiem tra email/mat khau.
- Kiem tra ma OTP (neu co 2FA).
- Thu dang xuat va dang nhap lai.
- Lien he Admin de reset mat khau neu can.

### 6.2 Upload that bai
- Kiem tra dung dinh dang file.
- Kiem tra kich thuoc file.
- Doi ten file khong ky tu dac biet la.
- Thu tai lai sau it phut.

### 6.3 Ket qua review/AI chua phu hop
- Viet cau hoi ro rang hon.
- Bo sung boi canh hop dong/tinh huong.
- Doi chieu voi nguon dan chieu va nhan dinh chuyen vien phap ly.

## 7. Quy trinh van hanh de xuat cho doanh nghiep
Hang ngay:
1. Upload tai lieu/hop dong moi.
2. Review hop dong rui ro cao truoc.
3. Tao/duyet van ban tu template.
4. Dong bo ghi chu va thong tin phe duyet.

Hang tuan:
1. Ra soat cac hop dong dang pending review.
2. Kiem tra tai khoan va quyen truy cap.
3. Tong hop vuong mac AI de cai thien prompt/noi dung.

## 8. Kenh ho tro noi bo
Khi can ho tro, cung cap cho bo phan ky thuat:
- Anh man hinh loi.
- Buoc thuc hien truoc khi loi xay ra.
- Thoi gian xay ra loi.
- Ma hop dong/tai lieu lien quan (neu co).
